Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

MindHarbor is a set of ChatGPT conversation instructions (a skill / plugin) and the website mindharbor.fezone.tw, made by Fezone Ltd. ("we"). This policy explains what we collect and how you can control it.

Your conversations with ChatGPT

MindHarbor is only a set of instructions ChatGPT follows. It has no server that receives your conversations. Your chats and any journal entries stay in your own ChatGPT account and are handled by OpenAI under the OpenAI Privacy Policy. We cannot see or access them.

MindHarbor only asks ChatGPT to save a journal entry to a service you connected yourself (such as Notion or Google Drive) when you explicitly ask. That data lives in your account and is governed by that service's policy.

The feedback form on this website

If you use the feedback form, we store only what you choose to enter:

  • Your overall rating (one of three), your message, optional contact details, plus the time sent and the page language.
  • We do not record IP addresses, device information, cookies, or any tracking data.

Use: to learn whether MindHarbor works well and improve it, and to reply to you if you left contact details.

Storage and sharing: stored in Cloudflare's database service, readable only by us. We never sell or share it.

Retention: kept for up to 24 months, then deleted.

Please don't paste conversations, health information, or anything that identifies you into the form.

The website itself

The site uses no cookies, analytics, or ad tracking. Fonts are served by Google Fonts, so your browser downloads font files from Google. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may process connection logs for security and reliability.

Your rights

You can ask us to view, correct, or delete what you submitted through the feedback form. Because the form doesn't record who you are, tell us roughly when you sent it and what it said so we can find it.

Children and teens

MindHarbor is not designed for children under 13. Users aged 13 to 17 should use it with a parent's or guardian's permission, as ChatGPT requires.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the feedback form on this site (with contact details) or GitHub Issues.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
