What it does

Not a questionnaire, and not a therapy session. Just someone who listens and keeps up with the conversation.

Talks like a friend

From anime and everyday life to work and the bigger questions, with no modes to switch. It follows your pace and doesn't treat every sentence as a problem to fix.

Just switch on voice

With ChatGPT voice mode you can talk on the way home, while doing the dishes, or during a show, without typing.

Writes your journal

When you ask, it turns the conversation into a journal entry. It only uses what you actually talked about and never makes things up.

Get started

MindHarbor is a set of instructions for a ChatGPT Project. Set it up once and keep using it.

  1. Create a new Project in ChatGPTYou can call it MindHarbor.
  2. Copy the SkillOn GitHub, open skills/en/SKILL.md (or skills/zh-TW/SKILL.md for Chinese) and copy all of it.
  3. Paste it into the Project instructionsOpen the Project settings, paste it into Instructions, and save.
  4. Start a new chat, or switch on voiceTry “I'd like to chat while I watch something” or “Help me write down how today went.”

Privacy and boundaries

Talking about how you feel should feel safe. These principles are built into MindHarbor from the start.

  • It never saves your conversations or journal to Notion, Google Drive, or any other service without your clear permission.
  • Journal entries only use what you shared. It doesn't invent events that didn't happen.
  • Your conversations stay in your own ChatGPT account. MindHarbor itself collects no data. The feedback form on this page stores only what you choose to type.
  • MindHarbor is not therapy, diagnosis, or a medical service, and it can't replace professional help.

If you're struggling right now

If you're in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.
